The MacArtney Way

It’s not about pushing deals — it’s about understanding the client’s needs and being proud of what you offer. You won’t be handed a script and a ruthless quota – you’ll be trusted to build real relationships and deliver customised, integrated solutions that matter.

Small and big at the same time.

MacArtney is a global leader in underwater technology — but at heart, we're still shaped by our origins as a family business, founded in 1978 in the small Danish town of Hjerting.

You'll work in a close local team while collaborating with colleagues across the world.

It's the best of both worlds — personal, supportive, and globally connected.

Products you can stand behind

Selling is easy when you believe in what you sell. You'll be contributing to projects that matter — enabling renewable energy, ocean exploration, national defence capabilities, and more.
