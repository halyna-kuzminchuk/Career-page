ESG

Turning values into measurable action

Guided by our ambition to Act Global and Think Local, we aim to make decisions with a long‑term perspective and take responsibility for how we operate every day. Sustainability at MacArtney means creating value in a responsible and balanced way. We consider the environment, how we treat people, and how we run our business — not as separate topics, but as part of the same mindset.

Our sustainability work is structured through Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG). ESG gives us a clear framework for setting direction, turning strategy into action, and measuring our progress across the Group.
