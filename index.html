Why join us

We connect our values to everything we build — including your career.

MacArtney was founded in 1978 by Mac and Winnie MacArtney — a husband and wife who sold their home to build something they believed in. That leap of faith grew into a global leader in underwater technology, with and a presence in some of the world's most demanding sectors: Offshore Renewable Energy, Ocean Science, and Defence.

For more than four decades, we have developed technologies that support life and work beneath the ocean surface — from SubConn underwater connectors and fibre optic systems to advanced connectivity and intelligent winch solutions.

Today, MacArtney remains family-owned by Mac and Winnie's sons, Glenn and Marco. The founders' original values — entrepreneurial courage, technical ambition, and — continue to shape the company today.
